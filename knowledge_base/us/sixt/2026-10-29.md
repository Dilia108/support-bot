# SIXT FAQ (US)

## Cancel a booking

You can cancel on the SIXT website or in the SIXT app: sign in, open "My
Bookings", select "Cancel" and confirm. With the Flexi rate, cancelling is
free of charge up to the scheduled start of the rental; a no-show fee may
apply if you neither cancel nor pick up the car. With the Prepaid rate,
cancelling is free within 24 hours of booking, provided pick-up is still
more than 48 hours away. After that, a fixed cancellation fee is kept and
the rest is refunded; the exact amount is listed in the Rental Information
for your booking country. For a no-show, or a cancellation after the
scheduled pick-up time, there is no refund. Refunds usually take 5 to 10
business days.

## Change a booking

With the Flexi rate, changes are free up to pick-up. With the Prepaid rate,
date changes are possible and are recalculated at the current rate.

## Check booking status

Sign in on the SIXT website or open the SIXT app and go to "My Bookings" to
see your booking.

## Late return

If you return the car later than agreed in the rental contract, the
additional days are charged as a rental extension. If you did not inform
SIXT in advance, an additional service fee applies. The easiest solution is
to extend the rental on the SIXT website or in the SIXT app as soon as you
know you will be late. SIXT's help pages do not say whether a grace
period applies to short delays.

## Pets in the car

Pets, such as a dog or a cat, are welcome in SIXT rental cars. They must be
secured and under control at all times, for example in a transport crate,
with a harness or behind a boot divider. Remove pet hair and coarse dirt
before the end of the rental: a cleaning fee may apply for heavy soiling.
SIXT's pages give no amount. For service animals of customers with
disabilities, SIXT waives the special cleaning fee.
