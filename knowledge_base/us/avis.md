# Avis FAQ (US)

## Cancel a booking

Pay-at-counter reservations can be cancelled without a cancellation fee.
For prepaid reservations, a $50 fee applies if you cancel more than 24
hours before pick-up, and a $150 fee applies if you cancel within 24 hours
of pick-up. If you do not cancel before the pick-up time and do not pick up
the car, no part of the prepaid amount is refunded. You can cancel online
at avis.com.

## Change a booking

You can change a reservation online at avis.com. Any change may affect your
rate. To extend a rental after pick-up, contact an Avis reservation agent
at 1-800-230-4898. At U.S. locations a service charge of up to $15 applies
to an extension, and your original per-day rate may change.

## Check booking status

You can look up your reservation on avis.com, or see it in your My Avis
profile after logging in.

## Late return

There is a 29-minute grace period for returns. After 30 minutes late,
hourly charges may apply. After 90 minutes late, a full-day late charge may
apply. If you know you will be late, contact Avis to extend the rental: if
you keep the car longer without telling Avis, a late charge of up to $15
per day can apply.
