# Enterprise FAQ (US)

## Cancel a booking

If you did not prepay, no cancellation fee applies. For prepaid
reservations: if you cancel more than 24 hours before pickup, you receive a
full refund minus a cancellation fee of $50. If you cancel less than 24
hours before pickup, you receive a full refund minus a cancellation fee of
$100. If you do not pick up a prepaid rental on the pickup date, you
receive a full refund minus a no-show fee of $100. You can cancel online
with your confirmation number, first name and last name. Reservations made
through a third party must be cancelled on the website where you booked.

## Change a booking

Before pickup, you can modify your reservation online with your
confirmation number, first name and last name. Modifying dates or times
may alter the daily rate. After pickup, contact the location where you
picked up the vehicle.

## Check booking status

You can view your reservation online by entering your confirmation number,
first name and last name.

## Late return

There is generally a 29-minute grace period for daily rentals at Enterprise
U.S. locations. If you return the vehicle within 2 1/2 hours of the
original rental time on a subsequent day, hourly charges apply. From 2 1/2
hours or more, you are charged for an additional day of rental. A vehicle
returned after the branch has closed is not checked in until the next
business day, and you remain responsible for it until then.
