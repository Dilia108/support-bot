## Known gaps in the published information

- **SIXT prepaid cancellation fee:** the help pages say a fixed fee applies
  but give no amount. The amounts are in SIXT's "Rental Information" pages,
  which could not be read automatically. Add them by hand per country.
- **SIXT late return, US and DE:** the help pages do not say whether a
  grace period applies. Only the Spanish page states one (30 minutes). The
  FAQ files say "not stated" on purpose; this must not be read as "there
  is no grace period".
- **Avis DE cancellation:** the FAQ gives no deadlines or amounts and
  points to the booking conditions of each reservation.
- **Enterprise ES prepaid cancellation between 7 days and 24 hours:** a fee
  applies, but the amount is only given by phone.
- **Avis US late charge of up to $15 per day:** Avis states this for
  renters who do not notify Avis of a longer rental; the FAQ wording ties
  it to notifying within 7 hours of the scheduled return.
- **Not covered at all:** pets, additional drivers, deposits, fuel, age
  limits. Questions on these are recorded as knowledge gaps.
